Honest by design

Predictions are not promises

Farming advice that hides its uncertainty is dangerous. KrushiMitra AI shows model error margins, says when data is missing, never invents a price or a cost, and never guarantees profit. Costs it can't know — seeds, labour, pesticides — only count when you enter them.
