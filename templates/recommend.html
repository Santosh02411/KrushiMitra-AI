Where the numbers come from

  1. Soil values (N/P/K/pH) — you enter these from a soil test; no API can guess what is in your specific field.
  2. Location — read from your device's actual GPS via the browser, then reverse geocoded to a place name. Far more accurate than IP-based location, which in India can be off by hundreds of km.
  3. Climate values — fetched live from Open-Meteo for your coordinates.
  4. Crop model — a RandomForest classifier trained on a published 2,200-row dataset, evaluated on a held-out 20% test split.
